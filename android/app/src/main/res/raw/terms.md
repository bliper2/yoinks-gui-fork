## 3. No bypassing of protection

Yoinks does not bypass DRM, encryption, paywalls, or other technical protections, and you must not use it to try. If content is protected, Yoinks will refuse or fail.

Private content stays private: for example, private Snapchat snaps and chats cannot be downloaded.

Spotify links work differently: Yoinks never downloads anything from Spotify. It only reads a song's public name, artist and cover, then looks for the same song on YouTube Music and downloads that, if it is available there.

## 4. Cookies (optional)
