## 3. No bypassing of protection

Yoinks does not bypass DRM, encryption, paywalls, or other technical protections, and you must not use it to try. If content is protected, Yoinks will refuse or fail.

Spotify links work differently: Yoinks never downloads anything from Spotify. It only reads a track's public name, artist and cover, then looks for the same song on YouTube Music and downloads that, if it is available there.

## 4. Browser cookies (optional)

If you turn on "Use browser cookies" in Settings, yt-dlp uses your own login from your browser to download videos that need you to be signed in (for example age-restricted videos). Only use this with your own account and for content you are allowed to access.
